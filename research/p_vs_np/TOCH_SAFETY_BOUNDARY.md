# TOCH theorem boundary

The predecessor corpus is a source of **candidate mathematical structure**, not authority.

A historical claim may contribute:
- a representation grammar,
- a counterexample family,
- a resource invariant,
- a proof-search obligation,
- or an adversarial gate.

It may not contribute a `SAT`, `UNSAT`, `P=NP`, or `P!=NP` conclusion without an independently replayable derivation in the current formal model.

`P_VS_NP = OPEN`.
